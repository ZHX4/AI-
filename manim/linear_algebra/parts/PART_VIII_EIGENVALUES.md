# Part VIII — Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

A deep visual chapter on eigenvalues, eigenvectors, eigenspaces, multiplicity, diagonalization, matrix powers, and dynamical interpretation.

## Lessons

1. Eigenvector intuition — invariant directions.
2. The eigenvalue equation — from `Av = lambda v` to `(A-lambda I)v = 0`.
3. Characteristic polynomial — roots give eigenvalues.
4. Finding eigenvectors — solve the corresponding null-space problems.
5. Geometric eigenspaces — every scalar multiple forms an invariant subspace.
6. Algebraic vs geometric multiplicity — repeated eigenvalues and defective matrices.
7. Diagonalization — change to an eigenvector basis.
8. Matrix powers — repeated transformations become scalar powers.
9. Dynamics and eigen-directions — dominant eigenvalues and long-term behavior.
10. Eigenvalues beyond 2D — the same structure in higher dimensions.
11. Eigenvalue mastery — synthesis of geometry, algebra, and dynamics.

## Teaching standard

Each lesson follows intuition → geometric construction → CC-style explanation → algebra → worked example → interpretation → recap. The canonical matrix

`A = [[3,1],[0,2]]`

is used through the core derivations so the viewer can track one coherent example across the chapter.

A repeated-eigenvalue diagonal matrix and a Jordan block are introduced separately to explain why algebraic and geometric multiplicity can differ and why diagonalization can fail.
